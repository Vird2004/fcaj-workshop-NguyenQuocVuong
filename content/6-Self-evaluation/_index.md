### Needs Improvement

* Build deeper professional knowledge and skills, particularly the ability to apply theory to real-world situations.
* Strengthen problem-solving thinking by proactively proposing more creative solutions instead of relying on familiar patterns.
* Improve communication and present ideas more clearly and coherently in day-to-day work.
* Increase the effectiveness of contributions to projects/the organization through more concrete improvement initiatives.
* After attending the meeting on May 30th and learning from the sharing sessions by Quynh Nhu and Khac Uy, I realized that I need to learn to be more confident and constantly strive not to procrastinate, in order to improve myself further. Most specifically, I need to make an effort to ask the admins and mentors more often because I am quite shy and introverted.

### Personal Sharing
After my internship period at AWS, I feel quite lucky to have experienced multiple roles such as DevOps, Cloud Engineer, AI Engineer, etc. Initially, I only thought I would work as a Software Engineer, doing backend tasks behind the scenes, and I was quite hesitant, thinking that if I took on other roles, I would have to deal with this and that. But then, after listening to the seniors sharing, I realized, "Why not?" I should just give it a try and see how far I can go. If it doesn't work out, I can always go back to being a Software Engineer or try something else to see if I find it suitable. I also gained some experience using several AWS services, exploring how things work, and figuring out alternative solutions to make the project function properly when a service didn't work. I hope that after this internship period, I can still stay involved because even though I may no longer be an official intern, thanks to this opportunity, I have gained another path and another choice, giving me more decisions to make and more motivation. Thank you all very much!
